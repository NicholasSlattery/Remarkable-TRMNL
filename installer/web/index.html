Step one — connect

Connect your reMarkable Paper Pro

Plug the tablet into this computer with USB, enable SSH/developer access in Settings, and enter the key shown by the tablet. Nothing changes on the tablet until you click Install.

Developer Mode is required

Enabling it factory-resets the Paper Pro, weakens secure-boot protections, and can affect support or warranty coverage. Back up and sync your files first. Restoring the normal interface below does not disable Developer Mode.
