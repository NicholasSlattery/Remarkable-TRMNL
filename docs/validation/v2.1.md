## Known limitations

- reMarkable OS 3.26.x remains installer-allowed but has not been exercised on
  hardware. Only 3.27.3.0 has.
- A multi-day battery runtime figure cannot be produced during a release; the
  in-app battery test is the supported way for an owner to measure their own.
- The colour palette used for dithering is an approximation of the panel's
  displayable set. It is overridable via `dither_palette` in `config.json` for
  anyone who measures a better one.
- The tablet cannot update itself. The update check only reports that a newer
  release exists; installing it still needs the Windows installer.
